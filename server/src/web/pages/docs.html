1

Check your PC

Luma Arcade runs on the PC you already game on. If it plays modern games, it almost certainly has everything below.

A Windows PC

Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Windows Server 2022 or 2025 works too. You need an administrator account to install.

A graphics card that can encode video

NVIDIA (NVENC), AMD (VCE/VCN), or Intel Quick Sync on 6th-gen Core or newer. Nearly every gaming PC has one. Setup checks for you.

Internet while installing

Setup downloads Sunshine, ES-DE and the emulators from their official releases. For playing, plan on about 10 to 15 Mbps of upload per player.

Friends: just a browser

On a phone, tablet, laptop or TV. Chrome or Edge work best. Nothing to install on their side.

To play away from home optional

A free Cloudflare account and a domain name you own (any registrar; often about $10 a year). No router settings, no opened ports. Without it, everything still works on your home network, and you can add it later.

Games you own

Steam and other launchers work as they are. For retro consoles you add your own games (ROMs) and BIOS files. Luma Arcade ships no games.

2

Run Setup

Setup asks a few questions first, then installs everything in one go. Nothing is changed on your PC until you press Install on the last page.

Download and open it

  1. Download Setup

    LumaArcadeSetup.exe from the latest release, on the gaming PC itself.

  2. Double-click it

    Windows may show a blue box: "Windows protected your PC". That's because Setup isn't code-signed yet, not because something's wrong. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Say yes to the admin question

    Windows asks whether Setup may make changes. Click Yes. It needs admin rights to install drivers and services.

The fast path. On almost every page the answer is just Next. The pages that need you: 8 (a password for the games account), 9 (your own name and password) and 10 (leave it as it is unless you have a Cloudflare token). Page 6 too, if the PC has no monitor.

The Setup pages, one by one

The numbered markers show what to look at. If a box is greyed out with "already installed", Setup found it on your PC and skips it.

Four pages need nothing from you. Welcome (page 1), License (2), the Luma Arcade folder (12) and You're all set (14). Click Next, I Agree, Next and Finish.

1 · Next 2 · I Agree 12 · Next 14 · Finish

After Finish: if you made a separate games account, restart the PC (or sign in to that account once), and keep your own account signed out while people play.

This PC's hardware

Which Windows account runs the games?

Whoever is streaming sees and controls that account's desktop. A separate account keeps your files, browser and saved passwords out of reach.

  1. A separate account just for games. Setup makes it for you, named "Arcade".
  2. Give it a password (twice). You won't type it day to day.
  3. Sign in automatically lets the arcade come back on its own after a restart.
The defaults: a separate account, signing in automatically.

Page 9 of 14

Your admin account

  1. The name and password you'll sign in to Luma Arcade with. At least 8 characters; make it strong if the arcade will be on the internet.
  2. Keep this ticked: Setup gives Sunshine the same sign-in and links the two, which saves three fiddly steps later.
Choose a name and password, and write them down.

  1. HTTPS on the home network. Browsers only allow controllers and full screen on secure pages, so keep it ticked.
  2. Tunnel token for playing away from home. Leave it empty for now if you like. Getting a token is explained below.
Keep HTTPS on. Paste a token only if you have one.

Games folder

ES-DE, the emulators and your retro games live here, so pick a drive with plenty of space. Every account on the PC can use it.

C:\Games, or a bigger drive like D:\Games. Then Install.

Installing takes a while: most of it is downloading. Leave the PC on and let it finish.

3

Sign in on the PC

Open a browser on the gaming PC and go to http://localhost:7777. Sign in with the admin name and password from page 9.

What's already done for you

  1. Sunshine is linked

    Setup paired Luma Arcade with Sunshine, so the PC shows up ready to play.

  2. Your games are listed

    ES-DE (retro consoles), Steam Big Picture and the Desktop appear as things to play.

  3. It starts by itself

    Luma Arcade and Sunshine start whenever the games account signs in.

Skipped the admin account, or the PC isn't listed?

Pair them by hand, once:

1. Open https://localhost:47990 on the PC (accept the certificate warning) and choose a name and password for Sunshine.

2. Open http://localhost:7777 and create an account. The first account becomes the admin.

3. In Luma Arcade, add a PC with the address localhost. It shows a PIN: type it into Sunshine's page under PIN.

4

Add your games

PC games need nothing extra. Retro games go in folders, one per console.

PC games

Anything installed through Steam shows up in Steam Big Picture. Epic, GOG and other launchers work from the Desktop, or add them to Steam as non-Steam games so they appear in Big Picture.

Retro games

Put each game file in ES-DE\ROMs\ under the console's short name. ES-DE offers to create these folders the first time it starts. Restart ES-DE after adding games and they appear.

A file called "READ ME - games setup.txt" in your games folder lists exactly what Setup installed and where everything goes.

5

Play from other devices

There are two addresses: one for devices in your house, and one for friends anywhere.

No domain? Do "At home" and stop there. Everything works inside your house; you can add "Away from home" any time.

At home

  1. Find the PC's name

    On the gaming PC: Settings > System > About. It's the "Device name". The IP address works too.

    Device specifications
    Device nameGAMINGPC
    Processor…

  2. Open the secure address

    On the TV, laptop or phone, type https://<PC name>:7778, for example https://gamingpc:7778.

  3. Get past the warning, once per device

    The browser says the connection isn't private, because the certificate is made by your own PC. Click Advanced, then Continue (Edge) or Proceed (Chrome). That's safe here: the address is your own PC, on your own network.

Want the warning gone for good on a device? Open http://<PC>:7777/luma-arcade.cer on it and install the certificate. Plain http://<PC>:7777 also works, but browsers block controllers and full screen there.

Checkpoint. On your phone, open https://<PC name>:7778. If you see the Luma Arcade sign-in page, home play works. Playing only at home? You're done: skip to inviting friends.

Away from home

Only playing at home? Skip to inviting friends.

Done once, on any computer, on Cloudflare's website. It needs a domain you own (any registrar, about $10 a year). It's the fiddliest part of the guide, so it's fine to ask a techy friend. Cloudflare moves its menus around now and then; the names stay close to these.

  1. Put your domain on Cloudflare

    Sign up at cloudflare.com (free) and add your domain. Cloudflare tells you two "nameservers" to set where you bought the domain. It can take an hour to switch over. Already on Cloudflare? Skip this.

  2. Create a tunnel

    Open Zero Trust, then Networks > Tunnels, and click Create a tunnel. Choose Cloudflared and name it anything, like luma-arcade.

  3. Copy the token

    Cloudflare shows an install command for Windows. Don't run it. Copy only the long part after --token, then paste it on Setup's page 10. Already installed? Run Setup again, choose Update, and paste it there.

  4. Point a public hostname at the PC

    Next, add a public hostname: a subdomain you like, your domain, and the service HTTP with URL localhost:7777. Save. That's the address you send friends.

No ports are opened on your router: the PC connects out to Cloudflare, and friends come in through that connection.

6

Invite friends

Three ways in, depending on how often someone plays.

  • Regulars: make them an account

    As the admin, create an account and send them the address, their name and a password. You can set play-time limits and which games each person sees.

  • Once in a while: send a guest link

    No account needed. It works for the time you choose and ends by itself. You can see who's using one, message them, or switch it off.

  • Right now: invite them into your game

    While playing, open People and invite someone straight in. Up to four players; their controller becomes the next player. Or make a link right there.

On a phone, the screen is the controller

Bluetooth and USB gamepads work in the browser too. Turn the phone sideways for the full picture.

7

Updates and extras

  • Updating: run the new Setup

    Download the latest Setup and run it. It sees the arcade and offers Update, Repair or Uninstall. Accounts, settings, games and saves are kept.

  • Private mode is on to start

    New arcades don't take account requests from visitors and store nothing from them. To let people ask for an account, turn it off in Settings.

  • Extra seats, for more people at once

    Settings > Extra seats: "Get this PC ready", then "Add a seat" with a Windows 10 or 11 Pro .iso. Each seat needs about 6 GB of memory and builds itself in about an hour.

8

If something's wrong

Start with Settings > Host health on the arcade. It checks Sunshine, the drivers and the helper scripts, and most problems show up there with a fix.

"Windows protected your PC" when opening Setup

Expected for now: Setup isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Download it only from the GitHub releases page.

Black screen, or the stream stops straight away

Nobody is signed in on the PC itself. Sunshine can only capture a signed-in desktop. Restart the PC so the games account signs in by itself, or sign in to it.

If you use Remote Desktop, connect with mstsc /admin. A normal Remote Desktop connection opens a separate session and leaves the PC's own screen empty.

Controllers do nothing in games

Open the arcade on the secure address (https://…:7778, or your tunnel address): browsers ignore controllers on plain http.

On Windows Server, there's no Xbox 360 controller driver by default. Re-run Setup, choose Update, and tick the Xbox 360 driver on the Controllers page, or choose PlayStation 4 controllers.

The certificate warning keeps coming back

Install the arcade's certificate on that device from http://<PC>:7777/luma-arcade.cer. Or use the Cloudflare address, which has a normal certificate.

The picture is blocky or stutters

Luma Arcade lowers the quality by itself when the connection dips, then raises it again. Wire the gaming PC to the router if you can: Wi-Fi on the PC's side matters most. Allow about 10 to 15 Mbps of upload per player.

Other programs stop starting after a restart

Windows signs in only one account by itself. Programs that need their own signed-in desktop won't start if the games account now owns it.

Still stuck?

In Settings > Host health, click Copy diagnostics (names and IP addresses are taken out) and paste it into a bug report on GitHub.

Start on the gaming PC: download Setup.
