The journey

From your button to the game, and back

The games run on Luma's PC. Your browser sends it your controls and gets a live video of the game back - many times a second, in both directions at once.

Your controls going to the PC Video and sound coming back
1 Your device

Just a web page

Your browser decodes the video with your device's own hardware, plays the sound, and reads your controller, keyboard, mouse or the on-screen touch controls.

2 Luma Arcade

The front door

Checks who you are and whether it's your turn, enforces play time, passes messages around, and keeps the PC tidy between players. Everything is encrypted on the way.

3 Stream client

The live link

Sets up a direct, low-latency connection to your browser (WebRTC). If a direct route isn't possible it goes through a relay, and as a last resort over a regular web connection.

4 Sunshine

Capture and compress

Grabs every frame the PC draws, and the graphics card compresses it (H.265 or H.264) in a few milliseconds. Your controls arrive as a virtual controller plugged into the PC.

5 The games

A real PC, running for you

ES-DE is the console-style menu. It starts the right emulator for each system - from NES to PS4, Switch and Xbox 360 - or Steam games, full screen.

6 And back

The round trip

Button press to new frame on your screen typically takes tens of milliseconds at home, a little more over the internet. A wired connection helps the most.

Click to game

What happens when you press Play

A lot gets done in the few seconds before the picture appears.

  1. Sign in

    With the name Luma gave you, or a guest link.

  2. Pick an app

    ES-DE for consoles, Steam Big Picture for PC games.

  3. Quick speed test

    About a second, to pick a starting picture quality.

  4. The PC gets ready

    Loads your saves, resizes its screen to yours, and brings the game to the front.

  5. Play

    Quality adjusts itself as you go. Home and window switching are one tap away.

  6. Leave

    Your game waits for you, and your saves are snapshotted when it closes.

Features

Everything the arcade does

Built for a shared PC that lots of people play on - at home and from anywhere.

Playing

Your controller, or touch

Plug in any controller - it joins on the first button press. On a phone, on-screen touch controls change layout to match the system you're playing.

Automatic quality

Watches your connection every two seconds and steps the picture up or down smoothly - without dropping your game.

Sized for your screen

The PC switches its own screen to your device's size and frame rate - up to 4K, and up to 240 fps at smaller sizes.

Home button

Back to ES-DE or Steam in one tap - keep the game running in the background, or close it.

Window picker

Hold Alt+Tab in the quick panel to see every open window on the PC and jump to the one you want.

Every console era

NES to PS4, GameCube, Wii U, Switch, Xbox and Xbox 360, handhelds, arcade, plus the PC's own Steam and Epic games.

Together

Co-op, up to 4 players

Invite people into your game from the People panel. Everyone plays on the same screen, each with their own controller.

Watch

Join as a spectator to see what someone is playing without taking a controller.

Guest links

A link that lets a friend without an account have a turn, or join a game, for a set amount of time.

Messages and announcements

News from Luma pops up on the home screen and in your stream, so nobody misses it.

A fair queue

Ask for the PC, wait in line, and get it held for you the moment it's free.

Your play stats

See your play time and top games for the week, and choose whether the welcome page shows what you're playing.

Looked after

Saves that are yours

Your game saves and ES-DE favorites stay separate from everyone else's, with snapshots you can go back to.

Play time limits

Daily and weekly limits where Luma sets them, with a warning before time runs out.

Lockdown

While anyone but Luma is playing, the PC's settings and admin tools close the moment they open. Games are all you can reach.

Health checks

The arcade keeps checking the streaming host, drivers, screen, backups and disk space, and fixes what it safely can.

Nightly backups

Every player's saves, snapshots and the arcade's accounts are backed up every night.

Picks up where you left off

Lost your connection? The game keeps running and stays yours for a while, so you can reconnect and carry on.

Taking turns

One PC, shared fairly

There's one gaming PC, so there's one player in charge at a time. Here's how the PC moves between people.

  • 1
    Ask, don't grabConnecting while someone plays sends them a request. They choose, and you see the answer straight away.
  • 2
    Wait in lineJoin the queue instead, and when the PC frees up it's held for you for 90 seconds while your page connects.
  • 3
    Walked away?After 15 minutes with no input, a request hands the PC over automatically.
  • 4
    Came back?A game left running stays yours for 10 minutes (3 if someone is waiting), so a dropped connection doesn't cost you your spot.

Your saves

Your progress is yours

Everyone plays on the same PC and the same emulators, but nobody overwrites anyone else's save.

  • Loaded when you connectMemory cards, in-game saves and save states, plus your ES-DE favorites and play counts, swap in before the game starts.
  • SnapshotsSave a snapshot of all your saves from the settings, and restore one when you need to. Up to 10 are kept.
  • Automatic ones tooWhenever a game closes, a snapshot is taken for you - separate from your own, so they never push yours out.
  • Your first timeYour first time, your saves start from the shared set the PC already had, and are yours from then on.

Safe by design

Private, secure and kept in order

Letting people play on a real PC means being careful about what they can reach.

A games-only Windows account

The streamed desktop belongs to a separate account with only the games on it. Nobody streaming can see Luma's own files, browser or passwords, and it can't change the PC.

Encrypted connections

The site and the stream are served over HTTPS through Cloudflare, so your sign-in and your game never travel in the open.

Accounts and roles

Everyone signs in with their own account. Admin tools are for admins only, and guesses at passwords are slowed right down.

Guest access that expires

Guest links carry their own time limit and end automatically. Luma can see who's using one, message them, or switch it off.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to install anything?

No. It all runs in a web browser - Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox - on a phone, tablet, laptop or TV.

Can I use my own controller?

Yes. Connect it to your device (USB or Bluetooth) and press a button once the stream starts. Xbox, PlayStation and most other controllers work. On a phone without one, use the on-screen touch controls.

Why does the picture sometimes get softer?

That's automatic quality stepping down so the game stays smooth when your connection struggles. It climbs back up once things are steady. A wired connection, or being closer to your Wi-Fi, helps the most.

What happens if my connection drops?

The game keeps running on the PC and stays yours for a while. Reconnect and you're back where you were.

Can a friend play with me?

Yes. Invite them from the People panel if they have an account. If they don't, Luma can make them a guest link.

Can other people see what I'm playing?

Only if you turn on "Show what I'm playing" in your settings - then the welcome page shows it. Luma can see play history as the arcade's admin.

How do I get an account?

Use "Request an account" on the welcome page. Luma gets your request and sets you up.

Ready to play?

Sign in to pick a game, or head back to the welcome page to ask for an account.
