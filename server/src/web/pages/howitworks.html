What happens when you press Play

A friend opens the link and lands in the lobby. One click on an app, and the PC does the rest while a short dialog counts the steps off. When they leave, the game waits for them.

Four seats, their own saves, and a fair queue

Up to four people play one game from one picture. Each keeps their own saves, with snapshots to roll back to. When more people want in than there are seats, the queue hands a seat over in order.

The picture steps up and down

Every 2 seconds Luma checks the connection and steps the picture up or down. A blocky moment beats a frozen one, and the game keeps running.

What to bring: one PC and about twenty minutes

PC
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or Windows Server. There is a Linux early beta.
GPU
One with a hardware video encoder: NVIDIA NVENC, AMD VCE/VCN, or Intel Quick Sync on 6th-gen or newer.
Setup
About 20 minutes, mostly downloads. Admin rights to install.
Picture
1080p60 at 10 to 15 Mbps is comfortable on a wired host with a modern GPU.
Friends
A browser on a phone, laptop or TV. Phones get on-screen touch controls, and gamepads work too.
Away from home
A free Cloudflare account and a domain you own. Optional: without it, the arcade works on your home network.
Under it
Sunshine encodes on your GPU, WebRTC carries it to the browser, and a Cloudflare Tunnel is the way in. No port forwarding, all open source.

Players can reach your games and nothing else on the PC

Everything a friend does goes through one encrypted tunnel and one front door.

A friend's browserPhone, laptop or TV, with a guest link or an account

Set it up in about 20 minutes

Run the installer on the PC with admin rights. It brings in Sunshine and a Cloudflare Tunnel. Point ES-DE at games you own, and Steam, Epic and the other launchers show up as apps. Then send your friends the link. Setup takes about 20 minutes, mostly downloads, and the guide walks through it.
