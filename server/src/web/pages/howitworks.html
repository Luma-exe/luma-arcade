01

The round trip of a button press

The frame leaves your PC, crossing capture, the live link and the front door. A friend presses A. The press comes back as a virtual controller, your graphics card encodes the next frame in a few milliseconds, and it goes out again.

05

Saves that stay separate

Everyone gets their own saves, with snapshots to roll back to. Players run as a games-only Windows account, so your own files are never on the menu.

06

A fair queue for one PC

The queue holds the PC for you and hands it over in order. Play-time limits keep one person from having it all night, and if your connection drops, the game stays yours for a while.

Features and security

features.txt
  • Any controller joins on the first button press
  • Touch controls change layout per console
  • Auto quality steps up or down every 2 s
  • Screen resize up to 4K, up to 240 fps at smaller sizes
  • Home button and a window picker (Alt+Tab)
  • Co-op up to 4 players, each on their own device
  • Spectate watch someone else play
  • Guest links that expire
  • Admin messages a note from the owner
  • Fair queue holds the PC for you
  • Weekly play stats
  • Per-player saves with snapshots
  • Play-time limits
  • Lockdown only games are reachable
  • Health checks and self-repair
  • Nightly backups
  • Reconnect the game stays yours for a while
  • Accounts and roles
  • Retro ES-DE and emulators
  • Modern Steam, Epic, Xbox app, EA, GOG, Ubisoft
security.txt
  • Encrypted from the browser to the front door.
  • The front door handles sign-in, turns, play-time limits and messages.
  • Games-only Windows account. Players can't see your files.
  • Lockdown mode: only games are reachable.
  • Guest links expire. Accounts have roles.
  • Cloudflare Tunnel is the way in.
