Your PC runs the game. Your friends open a link.

Luma Arcade turns a gaming PC you already own into an arcade for up to four players at once. They play from a phone, laptop or TV with nothing to install, and it works over the internet too, no port forwarding.

    Whatever is installed on your PC shows up in the lobby

    Luma ships no games. Friends pick from what your PC already has: Steam and the other launchers, plus retro through ES-DE and emulators. Every frame below is real gameplay.

    Steam, Epic, the Xbox app, EA, GOG and Ubisoft games show up as apps. Classic consoles run through emulators, for games you own.

    Install it on your PC, then send your friends the address

    1. Install it on the gaming PC. One installer, about 20 minutes, mostly downloads. The setup guide pictures every page.
    2. Pick the address. At home it's your PC's name. For friends anywhere, add a free Cloudflare Tunnel and a domain you own. No router settings.
    3. Send it. Friends open it in a browser. Up to four play, anyone else can watch.
