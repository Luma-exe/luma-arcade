    Why we built it this way

    1. 01

      It runs on your PC.

      Your games, your hardware, your saves. Nothing is rented, and nothing lives on a server you don't own.

    2. 02

      Only a browser.

      Friends open a link. No client to install, no store account, nothing to update. Phone, tablet, laptop or TV.

    3. 03

      Together, saves stay yours.

      Up to 4 players on one screen, each from their own couch. Every player gets their own saves, with snapshots.
