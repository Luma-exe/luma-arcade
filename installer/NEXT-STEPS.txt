Luma Arcade - next steps
========================

The full quick start (docs/QUICKSTART.md) is on GitHub:
https://github.com/Luma-exe/luma-arcade/blob/main/docs/QUICKSTART.md

1. Sign in
   Open http://localhost:7777 on this PC and sign in with the admin account
   you chose in Setup. Setup already paired Luma Arcade with Sunshine, so
   the PC's games (ES-DE, Desktop, Steam Big Picture) are ready.

   Skipped the admin account, or pairing didn't work?
   a. Open https://localhost:47990 on this PC (accept the certificate
      warning) and choose a name and password for Sunshine.
   b. Open http://localhost:7777 and create an account: the first one
      becomes the admin.
   c. In Luma Arcade, add a PC with the address "localhost". It shows a PIN:
      type it into Sunshine's page (https://localhost:47990 > PIN).

2. Play from other devices
   - At home: open https://<this PC's name or IP>:7778 in any browser. Each
     device warns about the certificate once: choose Advanced, then Proceed.
     Plain http://<this PC>:7777 works too, but without controllers.
   - Away from home: if you gave Setup a Cloudflare Tunnel token, add a
     public hostname to the tunnel in the Cloudflare dashboard (Zero Trust >
     Networks > Tunnels > your tunnel > Public hostname) pointing at
     http://localhost:7777.

3. Invite people
   Create accounts for friends in Luma Arcade, or make a guest link: it lets
   someone play for a time you choose, with no account.
   Luma Arcade starts in private mode: visitors can't ask for an account. To
   allow that, turn private mode off in Settings.

4. Games
   If you chose a separate games account, sign in to it once (or restart the
   PC if it signs in by itself) and keep your own account signed out while
   people play. Where games and BIOS files go: "READ ME - games setup.txt" in
   your games folder.

Updating: run the new LumaArcadeSetup.exe. It keeps your accounts, settings
and games.
