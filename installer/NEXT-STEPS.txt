Luma Arcade - next steps
========================

1. Sign in
   Open http://localhost:7777 on this PC and sign in with the admin account
   you chose in Setup. Setup already paired Luma Arcade with Sunshine, so
   the PC's games (ES-DE, Desktop, Steam Big Picture) are ready.

   Skipped the admin account in Setup, or it said pairing didn't work?
   a. Open https://localhost:47990 on this PC (accept the certificate
      warning) and choose Sunshine's admin name and password.
   b. Open http://localhost:7777 and create an account: the first one
      becomes the admin.
   c. In Luma Arcade, add a PC with the address "localhost". It shows a PIN:
      type it into Sunshine's page (https://localhost:47990 > PIN).

2. Play from other devices
   Browsers only allow game controllers and full screen on HTTPS pages (or
   localhost).
   - At home: open https://<this PC's name or IP>:7778 in any browser. The
     certificate is made for this PC, so each device warns about it once:
     continue anyway (Advanced > Proceed). To stop the warning for good,
     open http://<this PC>:7777/luma-arcade.cer on that device and install
     it as a trusted certificate.
     (Plain http://<this PC>:7777 works too, but without controllers.)
   - Away from home: if you gave Setup a Cloudflare Tunnel token, give the
     tunnel a public hostname in the Cloudflare dashboard (Zero Trust >
     Networks > Tunnels > your tunnel > Public hostname) pointing at
     http://localhost:7777. Then turn "session_cookie_secure" on in
     moonlight-web-stream\server\config.json if everyone plays through the
     tunnel (it stops sign-in working over plain http).

3. If you chose a separate games account
   Sign in to it once (or restart the PC if it signs in by itself): Luma
   Arcade starts with it, and Sunshine streams its desktop. Keep your own
   account signed out while people play.

4. On this PC
   Setup also installed Luma Arcade's helper scripts in
   C:\ProgramData\LumaArcade: lockdown while guests play, the stream page's
   Home button and window picker, per-player saves, game tracking and
   keeping ES-DE out of the way during games. Luma Arcade's Host Health
   (Settings, as the admin) checks them, Sunshine, the drivers and the
   virtual display.

Upgrading later: run the new LumaArcadeSetup.exe - it keeps your accounts,
settings and games.

Your games, BIOS files and emulator settings: see "READ ME - games setup.txt"
in your games folder.
