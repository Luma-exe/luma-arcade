Luma Arcade - next steps
========================

1. Sunshine's admin password
   Open https://localhost:47990 on this PC (accept the certificate warning)
   and choose a name and password. Only you need these.

2. Your Luma Arcade account
   Open http://localhost:7777 on this PC. The first account you create
   becomes the admin.

3. Pair Luma Arcade with this PC
   In Luma Arcade, add a PC with the address "localhost". It shows a PIN:
   type it into Sunshine's page (https://localhost:47990 > PIN) and the PC
   appears in Luma Arcade with ES-DE, Desktop and Steam Big Picture.

4. Play from other devices
   - At home: open http://<this PC's name or IP>:7777 in any browser.
   - Away from home: put Luma Arcade behind HTTPS, e.g. a Cloudflare Tunnel
     to http://127.0.0.1:7777 (then turn "session_cookie_secure" back on in
     moonlight-web-stream\server\config.json). Browsers only allow game
     controllers and full screen on HTTPS pages (or localhost).

5. If you chose a separate games account
   Sign in to it once (or restart the PC if it signs in by itself): Luma
   Arcade starts with it, and Sunshine streams its desktop. Keep your own
   account signed out while people play.

6. Windows Server
   Install "Xbox 360 Controller for Windows" from the Microsoft Update
   Catalog if you want Xbox controllers in games (or keep Sunshine's PS4
   controller emulation, if you picked it).

Your games, BIOS files and emulator settings: see "READ ME - games setup.txt"
in your games folder.
